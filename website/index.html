Installation Guide
Hisab is not on the Play Store. Google Play Protect may block the installation — this is normal for any sideloaded APK. Follow the steps below.
1

Download the APK

Tap the download button above. When prompted, confirm the download. The file will be saved to your Downloads folder.

2

Allow "Install from Unknown Sources"

When you open the APK, Android will ask for permission. Tap "Settings" then toggle on "Allow from this source" for your file manager or browser.

3

Disable Play Protect (if blocked)

If Play Protect shows "Blocked by Play Protect":

  1. Open the Play Store app
  2. Tap your profile icon → Play Protect
  3. Tap the gear icon (settings) in the top right
  4. Toggle OFF "Scan apps with Play Protect"
  5. Go back and try installing the APK again
4

Install & Open

Tap "Install" and wait a few seconds. Once installed, tap "Open" or find Hisab in your app drawer.

5

Re-enable Play Protect

For your security, go back to Play Store → Play Protect → Settings and toggle ON "Scan apps with Play Protect" again.

Architecture & Philosophy
[ 01 ]

Automated SMS Engine

On-device parser automatically reads incoming transaction SMS from your bank, extracts amounts, balances, and categories without any manual entry.

[ 02 ]

100% Offline Sanctuary

Zero cloud sync and no tracking inside the app. All SQLite database records stay strictly stored inside your private mobile device.

[ 03 ]

Biometric Security Vault

Native hardware biometric authentication with fingerprint and face scan, customizable auto-lock timeouts, and an instant privacy hide-balance mode.

Capabilities & Tooling

Unified Multi-Bank Financial Dashboard

Consolidate CBE, Bank of Abyssinia, Telebirr, Awash, Dashen, and custom accounts in one single clean timeline with real-time net worth calculation.

Spending Analytics & Expense Insights

Understand where your money goes with visual monthly expense curves, category distributions, income vs. spending ratios, and historical trends.

Custom Banks & Sender Rule Engine

Add your own banks, SACCOs, or fintech services with custom SMS sender IDs, user-defined regex rules, and custom institution branding.

Dual-SIM & Multi-Account Attribution

Separates transactions received on different SIM cards (subId tracking) and maps account number hints cleanly without cross-wallet balance contamination.
