Runs on your phone. Not on a server.

Your messages never leave the device. Hisab parses each balance SMS where it lands, keeps a running total, and gives you income and spending at a glance.

Balance stays on-device

Nothing is uploaded, synced, or shared. The ledger lives where your texts do.

Every bank that texts you

Awash, CBE, Dashen, and the rest — if a balance arrives by SMS, Hisab can read it.

Income and spending, plainly

See what came in, what went out, and what’s left — without opening a single app.

Why text messages?

Your bank already tells you your balance — dozens of times a day. Hisab simply turns those messages into a ledger. No new account, no password sharing, no internet banking login.
